01

Policy Statement

Saint Matthew's Schools are committed to providing a safe, secure, caring, and Christ-centred learning environment where every child is respected, valued, and protected from all forms of abuse, neglect, exploitation, violence, and discrimination.

The School believes that every child has the right to grow, learn, and develop in an environment free from harm. All members of the school community—including staff, volunteers, parents, contractors, and visitors—share responsibility for safeguarding children.

This policy is guided by the Constitution of Rwanda, national child protection laws, and international child rights standards.

02

Purpose

This policy aims to:

  • Protect every learner from abuse, neglect, exploitation, and violence.
  • Promote children's rights and wellbeing.
  • Establish procedures for preventing, identifying, reporting, and responding to child protection concerns.
  • Ensure that all staff understand their safeguarding responsibilities.
  • Foster a culture of safety and accountability.

04

Guiding Principles

Saint Matthew's School believes that:

  • Every child deserves dignity and respect.
  • The best interests of the child shall always come first.
  • Every child has the right to protection regardless of gender, disability, religion, ethnicity, nationality, or socioeconomic status.
  • Child protection is everyone's responsibility.
  • All allegations must be taken seriously.
  • Confidentiality must always be respected.
05

Definitions

Child — any person below the age of 18 years.

Child Abuse — any act or omission that causes actual or potential harm to a child.

Physical Abuse

  • Hitting, beating, burning, shaking
  • Excessive punishment

Emotional Abuse

  • Humiliation, threats, intimidation
  • Rejection, bullying, constant criticism

Sexual Abuse

  • Sexual touching, exploitation, grooming
  • Sexual harassment, exposure to pornography, rape

Neglect

  • Failure to provide food, medical care, education
  • Failure to provide supervision or shelter

Online Abuse

  • Cyber bullying and online grooming
  • Sharing inappropriate images
  • Internet exploitation
06

School Responsibilities

The school shall:

  • Promote a safe learning environment.
  • Recruit staff responsibly.
  • Conduct reference and background checks where possible.
  • Train staff on child protection.
  • Develop safe reporting systems.
  • Monitor student wellbeing.
  • Cooperate with relevant authorities.
  • Maintain confidential records.
07

Code of Conduct for Staff

All staff must

  • Treat children with dignity and respect.
  • Maintain professional boundaries.
  • Use appropriate language.
  • Avoid favoritism.
  • Supervise students adequately.
  • Report concerns immediately.
  • Respect confidentiality.

Staff must never

  • Use corporal punishment.
  • Humiliate learners.
  • Engage in sexual relationships with students.
  • Exchange inappropriate messages.
  • Be alone with a child in isolated areas without justification.
  • Give gifts intended to establish inappropriate relationships.
  • Use abusive language.
  • Discriminate against any learner.
08

Responsibilities of Parents

Parents are expected to:

  • Promote children's wellbeing.
  • Inform the school about any protection concerns.
  • Cooperate during investigations.
  • Respect school safeguarding procedures.
09

Responsibilities of Students

Students should:

  • Respect one another.
  • Report bullying or abuse.
  • Speak to trusted adults when worried.
  • Follow school safety rules.
  • Respect others' privacy.
10

Reporting Child Protection Concerns

Report immediately to

  • The Head Teacher / Principal
  • The Designated Child Protection Officer
  • School Management

External referral

Where necessary, the school reports to Rwanda National Police, child protection authorities, health professionals, and other competent institutions.

11

Responding to Allegations

When an allegation is received:

01

Ensure safety

Ensure the child is safe.

02

Listen carefully

Listen without leading questions.

03

Record facts

Record facts accurately.

04

Report

Report immediately.

05

Confidentiality

Maintain confidentiality.

06

Refer & cooperate

Refer to authorities and cooperate with investigations.

The school shall not conduct criminal investigations itself.

12

Confidentiality

Information relating to child protection shall only be shared with persons who need to know for safeguarding or legal purposes.

13

Anti-Bullying

The school has zero tolerance for:

BullyingCyber bullyingSexual harassment IntimidationDiscriminationHazing

All reports shall be investigated promptly.

14

Online Safety

The school shall:

  • Teach digital citizenship.
  • Monitor appropriate ICT use.
  • Promote responsible internet use.
  • Prevent online exploitation.
  • Encourage reporting of online abuse.
15

Recruitment of Staff

Before appointment, the school shall:

  • Verify qualifications.
  • Check references.
  • Require a declaration regarding child protection concerns where appropriate.
  • Provide safeguarding orientation and training.
16

Training

The school will provide regular safeguarding training for:

TeachersSupport staffSchool leadersVolunteers

Students and parents shall also receive child protection awareness where appropriate.
