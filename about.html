Our Origins

A Vision Born
in Faith

St. Matthew's School was founded in 1998 to offer Anglophone education to orphans of genocide survivors and children from underprivileged families in Cyangugu.

Initially, the school was established to serve these groups, but soon many local parents, who were neither genocide survivors nor impoverished, recognized the quality of education provided. They requested to enroll their children at Saint Matthew's School, which was later approved with a small contribution required as school fees.

"I did not build a school. I planted a garden where God's wisdom would take root in the hearts of Rwanda's children."

— Rt. Rev. Kenneth BARHAM, Founder

Our Journey

School Milestones

1998

School Founded

St. Matthew's School was founded in 1998 to offer Anglophone education to orphans of genocide survivors and children from underprivileged families in Cyangugu.

Initially, the school was established to serve these groups, but soon many local parents — who were neither genocide survivors nor impoverished — recognized the quality of education provided. They requested to enroll their children at Saint Matthew's School, which was later approved with a small contribution required as school fees.

Read more
2010

Expansion of Secondary Education

In 2010, St. Matthew's expanded its educational services with the establishment of St. Matthew's Lower Secondary School. This milestone marked a significant step in the institution's growth, enabling students to continue their academic journey within the same values-driven environment.

The introduction of lower secondary education strengthened the school's commitment to providing quality, holistic, and inclusive learning opportunities — preparing students with the knowledge, skills, and character needed for higher education and future leadership roles.

Read more
2019

Saint Matthew's Nyamasheke Campus

In 2019, Saint Matthew's School further expanded its educational mission with the establishment of the Saint Matthew's Nyamasheke Campus. The campus was founded to increase access to quality Anglophone education for children in the Nyamasheke community and surrounding areas.

Initially offering Nursery and Primary Education, the campus provides a strong foundation for young learners through a nurturing, child-centered, and inclusive learning environment — guided by the core values and educational standards of Saint Matthew's School.

Read more
2023/2024

Ntendezi & Gashonga Campuses

In the 2023/2024 academic year, Saint Matthew's School continued its growth through the establishment of the Ntendezi and Gashonga Campuses — inaugurated in response to strong requests from local communities seeking the high educational standards and values-based learning for which the school is recognized.

The opening of these campuses reflects the institution's dedication to serving families across the region and ensuring that more children have access to quality education closer to their homes, while upholding the mission and vision of Saint Matthew's School.

What Drives Us

Mission, Vision & Values

Our Mission

"To provide quality education rooted in faith, discipline, and academic excellence."

We cultivate environments where every student is empowered to discover their God-given purpose and achieve their full potential.

Our Vision

"To become a leading Christian school producing responsible global citizens."

We envision graduates who are intellectually sharp, morally upright, and spiritually grounded — ready to serve Rwanda and the world.

"The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom"

— Proverbs 9:10 · ST Matthew's School Motto

Core Values

The pillars that shape every student, every day

Faith

Our foundation. Every lesson, interaction, and decision is anchored in Christian principles and reverence for God.

Excellence

We pursue the highest standards in academics, character, sports, and spiritual life — in everything we do.

Integrity

Honesty, transparency, and moral uprightness define our students, staff, and institutional culture.

Discipline

Self-discipline and structured learning build the character that prepares students for life's challenges.

Innovation

Creativity and forward-thinking equip our learners to solve problems and thrive in a rapidly changing world.

Compassion

We nurture empathy, service, and love for others core virtues of our Christian community and Rwandan heritage.
