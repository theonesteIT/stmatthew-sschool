Conduct & Daily Life

School Rules

These standards guide behaviour, attendance, and respect across every ST Matthew's campus.

Attendance & Punctuality

  • Students must arrive on time for the start of the school day and all lessons.
  • Absences require a parent or guardian note; planned leave should be requested in advance.
  • Repeated lateness may lead to parental meetings and corrective measures.

Uniform & Appearance

  • Full school uniform is required on all school days unless leadership announces otherwise.
  • Uniforms must be clean, neat, and worn with pride — reflecting our values of discipline and respect.
  • Sports kit is worn only for PE, games, or authorised events.

Respect & Behaviour

  • Students treat teachers, staff, visitors, and peers with courtesy at all times.
  • Bullying, fighting, abusive language, or discrimination is not tolerated.
  • School property and campus grounds must be cared for responsibly.

Devices & Classroom Focus

  • Mobile phones and personal devices stay off and stored unless a teacher allows use for learning.
  • Students bring only materials needed for lessons and school activities.
  • Chewing gum and disruptive items are not permitted on campus.

Faith & Community Life

  • Participation in assemblies, worship, and value-based activities is expected with reverence.
  • Students uphold honesty, kindness, and integrity in every part of school life.
  • Parents are partners — concerns should be raised respectfully through the proper channels.

Discipline Process

  • Minor issues are addressed through guidance, warnings, and restorative conversations.
  • Serious or repeated offences may involve parental meetings, suspension, or further action.
  • Decisions aim to correct behaviour while protecting the learning environment for all.
