01

Policy Statement

Saint Matthew's Schools are committed to conducting examinations and assessments that are fair, valid, reliable, transparent, and consistent with the school's educational philosophy and the requirements of the Rwanda Basic Education Board (REB). The school seeks to promote academic integrity while supporting the holistic development of every learner.

02

Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to:

  • Ensure fairness and consistency in all assessments.
  • Measure students' knowledge, skills, and competencies accurately.
  • Promote honesty and academic integrity.
  • Guide teachers and students on examination procedures.
  • Ensure confidentiality and security of examination materials.
  • Provide clear procedures for handling examination irregularities.
  • Support continuous improvement of teaching and learning.

06

Examination Timetable

  • The examination timetable shall be approved by the School Administration.
  • Students shall receive the timetable at least one week before examinations begin.
  • Changes to the timetable shall be communicated promptly.
07

Responsibilities

School Administration

  • Approve examination schedules.
  • Ensure proper examination management.
  • Maintain examination security.
  • Address examination-related concerns.

Teachers

  • Prepare assessments aligned with the curriculum.
  • Submit examination papers on time.
  • Mark scripts fairly and accurately.
  • Return results within the prescribed timeline.
  • Maintain confidentiality.

Students

  • Attend all scheduled examinations.
  • Arrive at least 15 minutes before the examination begins.
  • Bring the required materials.
  • Follow examination instructions.
  • Demonstrate honesty at all times.
08

Examination Rules for Students

Students must

  • Wear the prescribed school uniform.
  • Sit only in their assigned seats.
  • Present their student identification where required.
  • Keep silence during examinations.
  • Raise a hand if assistance is needed.
  • Submit all examination materials before leaving.

Students shall not

  • Communicate with other candidates.
  • Possess unauthorized materials.
  • Use mobile phones, smart watches, or other electronic devices unless expressly permitted.
  • Copy from another student.
  • Allow another student to copy their work.
  • Leave the examination room without permission.
09

Academic Integrity

Academic honesty is expected at all times. Academic misconduct includes:

CheatingPlagiarismImpersonationCollusion Possession of unauthorized materialsTampering with examination scripts Obtaining examination papers illegally
10

Examination Irregularities

Any suspected examination malpractice shall be investigated fairly. Possible consequences include:

  • Warning
  • Cancellation of the affected paper
  • Award of zero marks for the assessment
  • Parent notification
  • Suspension from the examination
  • Other disciplinary measures consistent with the school's discipline policy

Students shall be given an opportunity to respond before disciplinary decisions are made.

11

Absence from Examinations

A student who misses an examination due to illness, bereavement, medical emergency, or other approved reasons must submit supporting documentation as soon as reasonably possible.

  • The school may approve a make-up examination where appropriate.
  • Students absent without valid reasons may receive a score of zero.
12

Students with Special Educational Needs

Reasonable accommodations shall be provided where appropriate, including:

Additional timeAlternative formatsReader or scribe support Accessible examination roomsOther approved accommodations
13

Marking and Moderation

Teachers shall:

  • Use approved marking schemes.
  • Mark objectively.
  • Moderate assessments where required.
  • Record marks accurately.
  • Maintain confidentiality.
14

Release of Results

  • Results shall be approved by the School Administration before release.
  • Report cards shall be issued at the end of each term.
  • Parents may discuss results during scheduled parent-teacher meetings.
15

Appeals

Students or parents who believe an assessment has been incorrectly marked may submit a written appeal to the Principal within five (5) working days of receiving the results.

The appeal shall be reviewed by the relevant Head of Department and the Principal. The decision shall be communicated in writing.

16

Confidentiality

Examination papers, marking schemes, scripts, and student results shall be treated as confidential documents. Only authorized personnel may access examination records.

17

Record Keeping

The school shall securely maintain:

  • Examination papers
  • Marking schemes
  • Student scripts (for the required retention period)
  • Assessment records
  • Examination reports
18

Policy Review

This policy shall be reviewed annually or whenever there are changes to national education regulations or school assessment practices.
