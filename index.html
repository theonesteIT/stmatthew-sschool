From Our Principal

Message from the Principal

A welcome from the leader guiding Saint Matthew's Schools with faith, excellence, and purpose.

MURAMIRA Benson

Principal · Saint Matthew's Schools

Welcome to Saint Matthew's Schools.

It is my great pleasure to welcome you to our school community. At Saint Matthew's Schools, we are committed to providing a holistic, high-quality education that nurtures academic excellence, Christian values, character development, and lifelong learning.

Our mission is to equip learners with the knowledge, skills, faith, and integrity needed to thrive in a rapidly changing world while remaining grounded in biblical principles. We strive to create a safe, inclusive, and supportive environment where every child is valued, challenged, and inspired to reach their God-given potential.

Through the dedication of our teachers, support staff, parents, and partners, we continue to foster a culture of excellence, innovation, discipline, and service. We believe that education is not only about academic achievement but also about developing responsible citizens and compassionate leaders who will positively impact their communities.

I invite you to explore our website and learn more about the opportunities, programs, and achievements that make Saint Matthew's Schools a place of growth, discovery, and transformation.

Thank you for your interest in Saint Matthew's Schools. We look forward to partnering with you in shaping the future of our learners.

MURAMIRA Benson, M.Ed

Principal
