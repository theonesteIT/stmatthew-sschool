What We Offer

Our School Levels

Nursery, Primary, and Ordinary Level Secondary three pathways united by excellence and faith.

Early Years

Nursery School

Play-based learning in a safe, joyful environment where young children build confidence, curiosity, and Christian character.

P1 - P6

Primary School

Strong foundations in literacy, numeracy, and values preparing pupils for success in upper primary and beyond.

Ordinary Level (O-Level)

Secondary School

Our Ordinary Level Secondary programme (From S1-S3) delivers rigorous academics, sciences, and spiritual formation to prepare students for national O-Level examinations and future studies.
